# Password Reset Policy

Document ID: KB-001
Owner: IT Service Desk
Last reviewed: 2026-11-01

## Who this applies to
This policy applies to all Nimbus Tech employees, contractors and interns who have a company account.

## How to reset your own password
Use the self-service portal at selfservice.nimbus.example. Choose "Forgot password", enter your employee ID, and confirm the one-time code sent to your registered email address. You can then set a new password.

## When the service desk can help
If you cannot reach your registered email, contact the IT Service Desk. They will verify your identity before any reset. Verification requires your employee ID and a one-time code sent to your registered contact details.

## Identity verification rule
No password reset is performed on the basis of a claimed job title or urgency. Seniority does not bypass verification. Requests that cannot be verified are declined and logged.

## Limits
- A maximum of 3 password reset requests is allowed in 24 hours per account.
- An account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
- A locked account can be unlocked through the self-service portal or by the Service Desk after verification.

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Longer passphrases are encouraged. Passwords do not expire on a schedule, but must be changed immediately if you suspect they were exposed.

## What IT will never do
The Service Desk never asks for your password, never asks you to read out a code you received, and never resets a password through an unverified chat message.
