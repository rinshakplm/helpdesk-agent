# Phishing Reporting Policy

Document ID: KB-003
Owner: Security Team
Last reviewed: 2026-11-01

## What counts as suspicious
Treat a message as suspicious if it creates urgency, asks for passwords or codes, contains unexpected attachments or links, comes from an address that looks slightly wrong, or claims to be from a senior leader asking for money or gift cards.

## What to do first
Do not click links, open attachments or reply. Do not forward the message to colleagues, because that spreads the risk.

## How to report
Use the "Report Phishing" button in your email client. If it is not available, send the message as an attachment to phishing@nimbus.example. You can also report it through the IT helpdesk chat.

## How reports are handled
Every phishing report is treated as a high-priority security ticket. The Security Team reviews reports within 1 hour during business hours and within 4 hours outside business hours.

## If you already clicked or entered your password
Act immediately. Change your password, then report the incident right away and say exactly what you clicked or entered. Do not wait to see whether anything happens. Fast reporting limits the damage.

## No-blame reporting
Reporting is never punished. People who report quickly, even after making a mistake, are helping protect the company.

## What IT will never do
IT and the Security Team never ask for your password or authentication code by email, chat or phone.
