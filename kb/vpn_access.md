# VPN Access Policy

Document ID: KB-002
Owner: IT Network Team
Last reviewed: 2026-11-01

## When you must use the VPN
All company systems accessed from outside the office network require the company VPN. This includes email, the intranet, shared drives and internal tools.

## How to install and connect
Install the VPN client from the Software Center. Open it, sign in with your company account, and approve the multi-factor authentication prompt. The client shows "Connected" when the tunnel is active.

## Session rules
A VPN session ends automatically after 12 hours and you must sign in again. Only one active VPN session is allowed per account at a time.

## If you cannot connect
1. Check that your normal internet connection works.
2. Close and reopen the VPN client.
3. Confirm your device clock is correct, because wrong time makes authentication codes fail.
4. Make sure your account is not locked.
5. If it still fails, create a ticket with your device type, the error message and the time it happened.

## If the connection keeps dropping
Move closer to your router or switch from public Wi-Fi to a mobile hotspot. If drops continue, report it with the times they occurred.

## Travelling abroad
Notify IT at least 5 working days before travelling outside India. VPN sign-ins from unexpected countries may be blocked automatically as a security measure.

## Public Wi-Fi
Always connect to the VPN before using company systems on public Wi-Fi such as cafes, airports or hotels.

## What IT will never do
The Service Desk never asks for your VPN password or authentication codes. VPN access is removed immediately when an account is disabled or an employee leaves.
