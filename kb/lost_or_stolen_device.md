# Lost or Stolen Device Policy

Document ID: KB-006
Owner: IT Security Team
Last reviewed: 2026-11-01

## Report immediately
If a company laptop, phone or tablet is lost or stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk and the Security Team straight away. Do not wait until the next working day. Speed limits the risk.

## What IT does after your report
Within 1 hour of your report, IT will remotely lock the device and, where required, wipe it. Your account sessions are revoked and your passwords are reset. Your MFA settings are reviewed.

## Personal devices with company email
If a personal phone has company email or apps installed, only the company data is wiped. Personal photos and apps are not removed.

## Theft
If the device was stolen, file a police report and keep the reference number. Share it with the Security Team.

## What not to do
Do not try to recover the device yourself from an unknown location. Do not keep using accounts that were signed in on the lost device without changing your password first.

## Replacement
A replacement device needs approval from your line manager. A temporary loan device may be issued while the replacement is arranged.

## Information to include in your report
State what the device is, when and where you last had it, whether it was locked, and what company data it may have held.
